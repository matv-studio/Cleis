# Cleis 0.2.3

**Install**: Cleis 0.2.2 offers this version itself (top-right button). Otherwise download
`Cleis-0.2.3-…-windows-x64-setup.exe` and run it (per user, no administrator). Your vault and its
backups stay where they are: an update never touches them. To start from scratch, use **Delete
account** (below).

### New
- **Sync with your Microsoft or Google account**: Settings › Account, choose Microsoft or Google
  then **Sign in**; your browser opens the provider's page and Cleis keeps your vault in a folder only
  it can reach (OneDrive's app folder, or Google Drive's hidden app data). On a new computer,
  **Sign in › Microsoft / Google** on the welcome screen brings it back, or creates your vault there if
  the account has none. **Revoke access** takes Cleis' access away. Signing in stays optional: a local
  vault needs no account.
- **Sign in with GitHub** without making a token: Cleis shows a code, **Open GitHub** copies it and
  opens github.com/login/device, and Cleis waits. The vault goes to a private repository
  `cleis-vault` of your account, the only one Cleis can reach (the "cleis-sync" GitHub App). When
  GitHub does not let Cleis make it, the dialog guides you: create it on GitHub's page, filled in for
  you, then install Cleis on it; Cleis notices and goes on by itself. The sign-in renews itself. On a
  new computer, **Sign in › GitHub** on the welcome screen brings that vault back.
- **A PIN for your vault** (Settings › Security › Unlock with): 4, 6 or 8 digits typed in boxes, on
  this computer only; after too many wrong PINs (3, 5 or 10), the master password or the recovery key
  is needed again. Also offered on the welcome screen. On macOS the PIN is tied to this Mac by a key
  kept in your login Keychain (on Windows, by your Windows account).
- **Keyboard shortcuts** (Settings › Shortcuts): every shortcut in one place, the system-wide ones
  (Quick Access, Fill best match) and those inside Cleis (lock, new password, search, copy, duplicate,
  move to trash). Press **Change** then the new keys; keys used twice are marked **In use**.
- **Folders named in place**: **+** next to My vault adds a row to type its name in (Esc or leaving it
  empty cancels); a double-click on a folder's name renames it.
- **Shortcuts under each vault** with the single Vault tab too (Preferences › Vaults › Shortcuts ›
  "Same for every vault" off).
- **Start in** a vault tab: My vault or an organization, when vaults have their own tabs.
- **Delete all backups**, and **Delete account** (vault, backups, settings and, if you want, the copy
  on GitHub or SFTP), at the bottom of Sync & backups; Cleis then starts again from the welcome screen.
- **Right-click on the password box** of the panel on the right: copy, paste, show, generate, previous
  passwords.

- **Back and forward** with the mouse's side buttons, or Alt+← / Alt+→ (⌘[ / ⌘] on macOS): the tab,
  settings page, list and item you were on.

- **Your account in the top right** when signed in: its picture, and a menu with Sync now, Account
  and Sign out.

- **No password, no recovery key forced**: a vault made with a PIN, Windows Hello or Nothing no longer
  makes you save a recovery key (you can make one later in Settings › Account). Synced with your
  GitHub account, such a vault opens on a new computer just by signing in to that account.

- **Organization access** (Security policy › Accessing <organization>): ask for authentication
  before the organization's spaces show — a password or a PIN set by an admin, or Windows Hello
  (Touch ID on a Mac) of each member's own computer — again after some hours or after inactivity.
  Works in a shared folder too (each computer checks it and counts wrong tries). Only a digest of the
  password or PIN ever leaves the admin's app.
- **Organization logo** (Server › Logo): shown when the organization asks for authentication, and at
  the top right while its vault is shown.
- **Delete server** (Super Admin, Server › Delete): erases everything the server holds, an encrypted
  export first if wanted; members' apps forget the organization at their next sync.
- **Touch ID** to unlock Cleis on a Mac (Windows Hello stays on Windows; neither is offered on Linux).

- **Contacts like the Vault**: My contacts and each organization are sections listing all their
  contacts, with their folders below; **Uncategorized** (contacts outside any folder) joins All
  contacts, Favorites and Birthdays soon. An organization's contacts can be added without a folder.
- **Health › Change** proposes a new password in a small card (edit it, ↻ for another, Apply);
  nothing changes before Apply.

- **Your profile** from the picture at the top right, signed in or not: name, e-mail and photo, kept
  in Cleis and shown to every organization you are in (members list, activity).
- **Rights per space**: in Settings › Spaces, click a space to see its members and set, for each one,
  Access, Edit and Show passwords. A space can only take away what the role gives.
- **Website column**: a button opens the site, as the copy button copies the password.

- **Recovery keys** for each password: paste the codes a site gives, mark the ones you used, copy
  one; a "Recovery keys" column (in Columns) shows how many are left.
- **Import by department**: a "Department / category" column puts each item in the folder of that
  name (a space in an organization), made if missing; columns Cleis does not know go to Notes; a row
  with several logins becomes one item per login.
- Right-click on **Trash** to empty it.

### Changed
- Sign-in with Microsoft accepts personal Microsoft accounts (Outlook.com, Hotmail) as well as
  work or school accounts of any company, when the server is set up for any tenant.
- Typing in a box saves it when you leave the box (or press Enter, or move to something else), not
  at every key; a sync no longer takes the keyboard away from the box you are typing in.
- New password, and the right-click menus, create in the organization shown (its section or one
  of its folders), not in your personal vault. Its logo stays at the top right in its folders.
- Preferences › Shortcuts: a grid, one row per shortcut and one column per vault, Contacts' below.
- An organization no longer decides how Cleis opens or when it locks: those settings are yours,
  for your personal vault too. "Unlock with" is now **Unlock Cleis with**.
- Accent colours are muted (violet `#494A6A`, and matching blue, green, teal, red); a colour picked
  from the former list moves to its muted one. Buttons show the colour as chosen; outlines and accent
  text stay readable on the page.
- Import & export of your personal vault stays under My account; each organization has its own
  (import into its spaces, export its sealed backup). The organization's Encryption page lists only
  its spaces.
- **One first screen in two steps**: Local account, Sign in or Join an organization; once one is
  chosen they move up and how Cleis opens appears under them (click an option to go back). Signing
  in with GitHub goes straight on: an account without a vault creates one here (then saved to a new
  private repository), an account with a vault opens it on the same card.
- **Settings › Account** now holds what Security and Profile were: opening Cleis, two-factor
  authentication (formerly "Verification code"), locking, clipboard, your organization profile, and
  **Delete account** at the bottom. **Revoke access** (when signed in to GitHub) forgets Cleis' access
  here and opens GitHub's page to remove it everywhere.
- "Master password" is now **Password**.
- Sync & backups: one **Sync** block; the place in use first, the others under a line.
- "Personal vault in its own tab" is only offered with an organization.
- Buttons show their hover and pressed states clearly with any accent colour.
- New default accent colour, a calmer bronze (`#977327`); a vault still on the former default ochre
  moves to it, another colour you chose stays.
- **New password** in place of New item.
- The top bar shows the connection as a dot alone; its tooltip says Local, or where the vault syncs.
- Renew soon is hidden from the sidebar by default (show it from its right-click menu).
- The padlock in the top bar is open when Cleis opens with nothing to type.
- A thin grey bar between column headings shows where to drag their width.
- Settings show no sentence under a choice any more: it is in the tooltip.
- **Right-click opens the menu only**: the row keeps the selection and the panel as they were, and is
  outlined while its menu is open; only a left click opens an item. Shortcuts pressed with the menu
  open act on that row.
- **Organization PIN** in boxes, as for your vault's PIN, sent as soon as the last digit is typed.
  Administrators set it in the same boxes, typed twice.

- Shortcuts: the keys sit in a box you click to change them; no more "Change" button.
- The copy and open buttons in a row show a stronger hover.

- Creating an organization: nothing is chosen at first; two choices, **Server** (on the NAS, a Linux
  server or this computer) or **Shared folder**; explanations moved to tooltips.
- The selected tab stays on top: the hover of the tab next to it no longer covers its outline.
- Contacts appear with the same short animation as passwords when you change folder.

### Fixed
- Your profile can be saved without an e-mail, with or without an organization; each organization
  keeps the address it has.
- Sign in with GitHub: after an expired or refused code the dialog offers **Try again** instead of a
  button that did nothing.
- A tab under the pointer no longer covers the outline of the active tab next to it (100, 125, 150 %).
- Welcome screen: the unlock choices ("Unlock Cleis with") go under their label when the line is too
  short for both, instead of running out of the card (French).
- Welcome screen: an option you can switch to takes the click and shows its hover on its whole card,
  not only on its title line.
- The accent line of a column's width handle now sits on the grey bar between headings.
