# Cleis 0.2.3

**Install**: Cleis 0.2.2 offers this version itself (top-right button). Otherwise download
`Cleis-0.2.3-…-windows-x64-setup.exe` and run it (per user, no administrator). Your vault and its
backups stay where they are: an update never touches them. To start from scratch, use **Delete
account** (below).

### New
- **Sign in with GitHub** without making a token: Cleis shows a code, **Open GitHub** copies it and
  opens github.com/login/device, and Cleis waits. The vault goes to a private repository
  `cleis-vault` made in your account the first time. On a new computer, **Sign in › GitHub** on the
  welcome screen brings that vault back.
- **A PIN for your vault** (Settings › Security › Unlock with): 4, 6 or 8 digits typed in boxes, on
  this computer only; after too many wrong PINs (3, 5 or 10), the master password or the recovery key
  is needed again. Also offered on the welcome screen. On macOS the PIN is tied to this Mac by a key
  kept in your login Keychain (on Windows, by your Windows account).
- **Keyboard shortcuts** (Settings › Shortcuts): every shortcut in one place, the system-wide ones
  (Quick Access, Fill best match) and those inside Cleis (lock, new password, search, copy, duplicate,
  move to trash). Press **Change** then the new keys; keys used twice are marked **In use**.
- **Folders named in place**: **+** next to My vault adds a row to type its name in (Esc or leaving it
  empty cancels); a double-click on a folder's name renames it.
- **Shortcuts under each vault** with the single Vault tab too (Preferences › Vaults › Shortcuts ›
  "Same for every vault" off).
- **Start in** a vault tab: My vault or an organization, when vaults have their own tabs.
- **Delete all backups**, and **Delete account** (vault, backups, settings and, if you want, the copy
  on GitHub or SFTP), at the bottom of Sync & backups; Cleis then starts again from the welcome screen.
- **Right-click on the password box** of the panel on the right: copy, paste, show, generate, previous
  passwords.

- **Back and forward** with the mouse's side buttons, or Alt+← / Alt+→ (⌘[ / ⌘] on macOS): the tab,
  settings page, list and item you were on.

- **Your account in the top right** when signed in: its picture, and a menu with Sync now, Account
  and Sign out.

### Changed
- **One first screen in two steps**: Local account, Sign in or Join an organization; once one is
  chosen they move up and how Cleis opens appears under them (click an option to go back). Signing
  in with GitHub goes straight on: an account without a vault creates one here (then saved to a new
  private repository), an account with a vault opens it on the same card.
- **Settings › Account** now holds what Security and Profile were: opening Cleis, two-factor
  authentication (formerly "Verification code"), locking, clipboard, your organization profile, and
  **Delete account** at the bottom. **Revoke access** (when signed in to GitHub) forgets Cleis' access
  here and opens GitHub's page to remove it everywhere.
- "Master password" is now **Password**.
- Sync & backups: one **Sync** block; the place in use first, the others under a line.
- "Personal vault in its own tab" is only offered with an organization.
- Buttons show their hover and pressed states clearly with any accent colour.
- New default accent colour, a calmer bronze (`#977327`); a vault still on the former default ochre
  moves to it, another colour you chose stays.
- **New password** in place of New item.
- The top bar shows the connection as a dot alone; its tooltip says Local, or where the vault syncs.
- Renew soon is hidden from the sidebar by default (show it from its right-click menu).
- The padlock in the top bar is open when Cleis opens with nothing to type.
- A thin grey bar between column headings shows where to drag their width.
- Settings show no sentence under a choice any more: it is in the tooltip.
- **Right-click opens the menu only**: the row keeps the selection and the panel as they were, and is
  outlined while its menu is open; only a left click opens an item. Shortcuts pressed with the menu
  open act on that row.
- **Organization PIN** in boxes, as for your vault's PIN, sent as soon as the last digit is typed.
  Administrators set it in the same boxes, typed twice (Security policy › Organization PIN › Change).

### Fixed
- Sign in with GitHub: after an expired or refused code the dialog offers **Try again** instead of a
  button that did nothing.
- A tab under the pointer no longer covers the outline of the active tab next to it (100, 125, 150 %).
