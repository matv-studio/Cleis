# Cleis 0.2.4

**Install**: Cleis 0.2.3 offers this version itself (top-right button). Otherwise download
`Cleis-0.2.4-…-windows-x64-setup.exe` and run it (per user, no administrator). Your vault and its
backups stay where they are: an update never touches them.

### New
- **Folder lines**: in All items, a vault, All contacts and My contacts, the items come folder by
  folder, each after a line with the folder's name (and its vault's, when the list mixes vaults).
  Preferences › Lists › **Folder lines** turns them off.
- **Folder icons**: right-click a folder › **Icon**, 30 icons, or Default. An organization's admins
  give its spaces an icon every member sees.
- **Clear the search** with the × at the right of the box, or Esc.
- **Type of an email or a phone**: a click lists every type to choose from.
- **Phone numbers** in each country's own format (France in pairs: 06 12 34 56 78, +33 6 12 34 56
  78; Switzerland +41 79 123 45 67…). A number typed without a country code is taken as French.
- **Birthday calendar** in Contacts, as for the vault's dates; **No year** keeps the day and month.
- **Billing period** of a price: a button at the right of the box, month and year in turn.
- **Scroll bars** on long lists and pages, and **middle-click scrolling**: press the wheel, move the
  pointer away from the anchor to scroll (faster the farther), click or Esc to stop.
- **Fill best match** on a site no item is saved for exactly: Quick Access lists the most likely
  logins first, by the same site (another address of it) or the site's name in the item's name.
- **Two-page sign-ins** (email, then password on the next page) fill in one go: Cleis sends the
  email, waits for the password page and fills it.
- Favorites show a full star after their name in the vault, as in Contacts.

### Fixed
- Contacts › Columns › **Website** now shows the contacts' Website column (it turned the vault's on
  instead). The same for Name and Notes, and for column widths and order: each table keeps its own.
- A click near the edge of a text box (search, panel boxes, settings) now lets you type; before,
  the box lit up but the keys went nowhere.
- In a window too narrow for every column, the columns get narrower in proportion to their width
  instead of running past the window's edge.
